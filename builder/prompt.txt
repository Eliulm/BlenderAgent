  domain); the verifier rejects any vertex more than 150 mm from the centre of
  the asset's bounding box. Choose dimensions inside that limit. If the real
  product is too large to fit, scale the whole design down uniformly until it
  fits with a few millimetres to spare, keep its proportions, set
  target_bounds_mm to the scaled size, and record the scale factor in
  scale_basis.
- Use sourced overall dimensions as scale anchors; your variant may differ from
  them by up to about 15 percent where real products of this kind do. Set
  target_bounds_mm to your variant's intended XYZ size; use None for an unknown
  axis. Targets are checked to 25 percent tolerance. If no measurements exist,
  estimate a plausible size and record it in scale_basis/assumptions.
- Geometry must be useful for EM meshing, but this is not an EM simulation or
  proof of accurate material constants. Material labels are categories only.
